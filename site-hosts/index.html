The place matters as much as the plug.

Retail, fuel, and hospitality properties can all be part of the conversation. We look at the whole stop, and this is what we look for.

Easy access

Straightforward to reach, enter, and move around, in a location drivers already have a reason to pass.

Useful amenities

Food, drinks, restrooms, or somewhere comfortable to take a break. The right mix depends on the property.

Hours that fit

Amenities that are open and accessible at the times drivers are likely to charge.

A cared-for property

Clean, well lit, and well maintained, during the day and after dark.

A host who looks after guests

Someone who takes responsibility for the property and the people who visit it.

A layout that works

Room for charging alongside your customers, deliveries, pedestrians, and accessible parking.

Your business. Our charging.

Rangeway’s role

Rangeway would develop and manage the charging operation and the ChargeVia customer experience.

Your role

You keep running the business your customers know, with the amenities and care that make your property a useful stop.

Each property needs its own review. Site fit, scope, and responsibilities would be agreed in writing before a project moves forward.

Have a place in mind?

A short note is enough to start. It helps if you include:

  • The business name and property address.
  • What is on site today, and when it is open.
  • A photo or plan of the parking area.
  • Who owns or controls the property.
