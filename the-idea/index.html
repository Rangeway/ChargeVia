Charging that fits the stop you would make anyway.

ChargeVia by Rangeway is being developed to bring fast charging to places that already give you a reason to stop.

Make the stop count.

A coffee. Lunch. A restroom break. Make the stop useful.

That is why we start with the property. We are looking for existing retail, fuel, and hospitality businesses with easy access, useful amenities, and people who care about the place.

Every property is different. One might have a market and a patio, and another a coffee counter and clean restrooms. What each one offers, and when it is open, depends on the host.

The stop that always works.

That is the standard we are building toward.

It means charging you can count on, a property that is cared for, and a useful way to spend the time.

Each location would have its own character. The care behind the charging experience should feel familiar wherever you stop.

By Rangeway

A charging stop with people behind it.

ChargeVia is Rangeway’s charging format for existing businesses. Rangeway would develop and manage the charging, alongside the hosts whose businesses make each stop useful.
